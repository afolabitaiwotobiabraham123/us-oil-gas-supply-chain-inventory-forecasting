# Materials & Inventory Demand Forecasting

This folder contains forecasting models developed to estimate future material requirements and support inventory planning within the oil and gas industry.

## Planned Forecasting Notebooks

1. `01_material_demand_analysis.ipynb` — Analyze historical material consumption, demand patterns, and variability.
2. `02_material_demand_forecasting.ipynb` — Develop forecasting models to estimate future demand for operational materials and maintenance supplies.
3. `03_inventory_replenishment_forecasting.ipynb` — Use forecast demand and available inventory information to support replenishment planning.
4. `04_safety_stock_and_reorder_point.ipynb` — Calculate safety stock and reorder points using suitable demand and lead-time assumptions.
5. `05_forecast_evaluation.ipynb` — Evaluate forecasting performance using appropriate error metrics and compare predicted demand against actual demand.

## Forecasting Objectives

- Identify historical material demand patterns.
- Forecast future material consumption.
- Support inventory replenishment decisions.
- Reduce the risk of stockouts and excess inventory.
- Improve safety stock and reorder point planning.
- Evaluate forecasting accuracy.

## Tools

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Scikit-learn
- Statsmodels

**Note:** Forecasting methods and inventory calculations will depend on the availability, quality, and suitability of the historical data. Forecast results will be evaluated before recommendations are made.
